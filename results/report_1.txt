7. CAVEATS / LIMITATIONS TO CARRY INTO THE MANUSCRIPT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - This comparison only includes rows where BOTH methods produced a plan; it does not by itself quantify Proposed advantage on the configurations where only Proposed succeeded (F2C's failure rate is reported separately in Section 1 using the full attempt log).
  - Wilcoxon tests here treat each row as one paired observation; Section 5's per-polygon aggregation is the safeguard against treating correlated same-polygon rows as fully independent, but a formal mixed-effects model (random intercept per polygon) would be a more rigorous treatment if a reviewer asks for one.
  - Tie tolerances (listed at the top of this report) are a modeling choice; the figures and win-rate numbers will shift slightly with a different tolerance, though the sign and significance of the Wilcoxon tests (which do not use the tie tolerance) will not.
